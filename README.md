# Connect your computer to GitHub — with your AI assistant

You don't need to know how to code, and you don't need to open a terminal
yourself. Your AI assistant does the setup for you.

By the end, your computer will be connected to GitHub: Git installed, an SSH
key created and registered on your account, your Git identity set, and the
connection verified.

## What to do

1. **Create a GitHub account** (skip if you already have one) at
   [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup).

2. **Open your AI assistant** — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or GitHub Copilot — and
   **give it access to this repository** (the same one where you're reading this
   file).

3. **Send it this message:**

   > Follow `AGENTS.md` in this repository to set up my computer for GitHub.

   *(If you also want to download a specific project, add: "then clone it from
   `<the project's SSH URL>`".)*

4. **Answer what it asks.** Your AI will need:
   - the **email** of your GitHub account, and
   - the **name** you want shown on your work.

   At one point it will show you a "public key" and ask you to paste it into
   your GitHub account in the browser, and to sign in / approve two-factor
   authentication (2FA). **Only you can do those steps** — your AI cannot log
   in to your account for you. Follow its instructions and then continue.

5. **Done.** Your computer is now connected to GitHub. Your AI can confirm it
   with the read-only check described in `AGENTS.md`.

## If something doesn't work

Tell your AI what happened, or paste the exact error message it showed you — it
can re-read `AGENTS.md` and walk you through the fix.
